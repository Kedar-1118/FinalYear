# New Research Papers (2020–2025)

This directory contains recent, peer-reviewed research papers (NeurIPS, ICML, AAAI, AISTATS, TMLR, arXiv) relevant to **accelerated decision tree/forest induction**, **multi-armed bandits (MAB) / best arm identification**, **finite-population concentration bounds**, and **tree ensemble optimization**.

For an in-depth academic synthesis, methodology breakdown, comparative taxonomy matrix, and technical mapping to our project, refer to:
👉 **[Comprehensive Literature Review (New Papers)](file:///k:/mega%20project/docs/literature_review_newPapers.md)**

---

## Index of Downloaded Papers

1. **[Tiwari et al. (NeurIPS 2022)](file:///k:/mega%20project/docs/newPapers/Tiwari2022_MABSplit_Faster_Forest_Training_Using_Multi_Armed_Bandits.pdf)**
   * *MABSplit: Faster Forest Training Using Multi-Armed Bandits*
   * Direct predecessor: frames node splitting as MAB with Hoeffding bounds.
2. **[Babbar et al. (ICML 2024 Oral)](file:///k:/mega%20project/docs/newPapers/Babbar2024_Near_Optimal_Decision_Trees_in_a_SPLIT_Second.pdf)**
   * *Near-Optimal Decision Trees in a SPLIT Second*
   * Proves greediness suffices near leaves; corroborates our Depth-Adaptive Hybrid Solver.
3. **[Tiwari et al. (NeurIPS 2023)](file:///k:/mega%20project/docs/newPapers/Tiwari2023_BanditPAM++_Faster_k_Medoids_Clustering.pdf)**
   * *BanditPAM++: Faster $k$-Medoids Clustering*
   * Computation reuse and multi-stage arm pruning in pure exploration bandits.
4. **[Sullivan et al. (AAAI 2024 Oral)](file:///k:/mega%20project/docs/newPapers/Sullivan2023_MAPTree_Beating_Optimal_Decision_Trees.pdf)**
   * *MAPTree: Beating "Optimal" Decision Trees with Bayesian Decision Trees*
   * Non-greedy tree search and structural properties under probabilistic formulation.
5. **[Waudby-Smith & Ramdas (NeurIPS 2020)](file:///k:/mega%20project/docs/newPapers/WaudbySmith2020_Confidence_Sequences_Sampling_Without_Replacement.pdf)**
   * *Confidence Sequences for Sampling Without Replacement*
   * Theoretical foundations for time-uniform finite-population empirical Bernstein bounds.
6. **[Liu et al. (arXiv 2024)](file:///k:/mega%20project/docs/newPapers/Liu2024_Superfast_Selection_for_Decision_Tree_Algorithms.pdf)**
   * *Superfast Selection for Decision Tree Algorithms*
   * Linear split-finding reduction using prefix sums on tabular datasets.
7. **[Liu & Mazumder (AISTATS 2023)](file:///k:/mega%20project/docs/newPapers/Liu2023_ForestPrune_Compact_Depth_Pruned_Tree_Ensembles.pdf)**
   * *ForestPrune: Compact Depth-Pruned Tree Ensembles*
   * Post-processing optimization and depth-layer pruning for tree ensembles.
8. **[Karthikeyan et al. (TMLR 2023)](file:///k:/mega%20project/docs/newPapers/Karthikeyan2023_Decision_Trees_with_Bandit_Feedback.pdf)**
   * *Learning Accurate Decision Trees with Bandit Feedback via Quantized Gradient Descent*
   * Convergence and sample efficiency under quantized representations and bandit feedback.
